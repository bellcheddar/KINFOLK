About

How the atlas was made

KINFOLK compares three views of every human kinase catalytic domain: experimental structures from the PDB (via KLIFS), the single AlphaFold DB model, and an ensemble of ESMFold2 diffusion samples. Everything on these pages was computed in advance on one Mac Studio; the site only serves the results.

A blind entry is sequence-novel when its chain shares under 40% identity (MMseqs2, at least half the query covered) with every protein chain released before the relevant cutoff.

Method notes

  • Unit of analysis: the catalytic domain, one row per domain (for example JAK2_JH1 and JAK2_JH2).
  • Superposition frame: the stable pocket core (KLIFS regions I, II, III, IV, V, gatekeeper, hinge, VI and VII; Cα only).
  • Kincore labels: the published spatial and dihedral rules, ported and checked against the Kincore database (19 of 20 chains agree).
  • Pocket fingerprints: KiSSim-lite, an 85-position physicochemical, exposure and subpocket-distance fingerprint following the KiSSim design (the kissim package itself could not be installed).
  • ESMFold2 settings: 3 recycling loops, 100 sampling steps, float32 on Apple MPS.
